hi, i'm jagoda ✦

I'm an AI researcher working on bioacoustics — teaching machines to listen to animals. Most of my time goes into recordings of frogs and dolphins, somewhere between deep learning, audio processing and ecology.

Right now I'm working on marine mammal acoustics at the Acoustic Research Laboratory, National University of Singapore.

This is my little corner of the internet. There's a room for my research, one for photos I've taken, and a bookshelf of things I've read, watched and loved. Make yourself at home.
