what i work on

I use machine learning to analyse acoustic recordings of wildlife. The questions are things like: who is calling, how many of them are there, and what does it tell us about the health of a habitat?

My interests: bioacoustics, biodiversity monitoring, AI, field research, and everywhere technology bumps into nature.
